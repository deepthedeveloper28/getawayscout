Affiliate & Search Disclaimer

Transparency is central to our mission at Getawayscout.com. In compliance with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines and international commercial disclosure standards, please read this full affiliate and search aggregator disclaimer.

1. Affiliate Disclosure & Commercial Partnerships

Getawayscout.com participates in multiple travel and retail affiliate programs designed to provide a means for independent publishers to earn advertising fees by linking to official travel merchants and verified retailers.

When you click on search links, widgets, banners, or buttons directing to external booking portals and partner storefronts (including, but not limited to, Amazon, Aviasales, Expedia Group, Booking.com, DiscoverCars, BikesBooking, KiwiTaxi, GetYourGuide, Tiqets, Compensair, Airalo, and GoCity), we may receive a modest referral commission from the merchant if you complete a qualifying purchase or booking.

Zero Extra Cost to You: Clicking our affiliate links or completing reservations via our search engines and shop recommendations never increases your price. In many cases, our direct partner integrations provide negotiated flash rates, early-bird promotions, and exclusive booking discounts.

2. Amazon Associates Program Disclosure

Getawayscout.com is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for website owners to earn advertising fees and referral commissions by advertising and linking to Amazon.com, Amazon.co.uk, Amazon.ca, Amazon.de, and affiliated international Amazon marketplaces.

As an Amazon Associate, Getawayscout.com earns from qualifying purchases. Product prices, availability, and Prime shipping eligibility displayed on our boutique shop and curated packing guides are accurate as of the date/time indicated and are subject to change. Any price and availability information displayed on Amazon at the time of purchase will apply to the purchase of that product.

3. Meta-Search Aggregator Role

Getawayscout.com is an independent meta-search platform and informational directory. We are not a travel agency, carrier, tour operator, or hospitality provider. We do not issue tickets, manage baggage, operate vehicles, or control room availability. All agreements for travel services are concluded solely between you and the respective third-party provider.

4. Price Accuracy & Currency Conversion

Prices and availability displayed on Getawayscout.com are pulled in real time from partner feeds and API databases. While we take every measure to ensure data freshness, airfares, room rates, and exchange rates fluctuate rapidly. Final total charges, including localized taxes, baggage fees, and mandatory resort fees, are determined on the third-party merchant's checkout screen before payment.

5. Editorial Independence

Our travel blogs, destination guides, and product recommendations are based on research, traveler reviews, and editorial integrity. Commercial partnerships do not dictate our editorial reviews or the order in which algorithmic search results are generated.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding our affiliate partnerships, pricing models, or site operation, please contact us at support@getawayscout.com or visit our Contact Us page.
