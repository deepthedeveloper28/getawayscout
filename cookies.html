Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Getawayscout.com uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you browse our travel comparison website. By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you visit a website. They help websites recognize your browser, remember your preferences (like language or currency), and provide a faster, personalized browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

Getawayscout.com utilizes cookies for several essential functions:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for website navigation, security, and loading search forms without errors.
  • Affiliate Attribution Cookies: When you click to book a flight, hotel, or rental car, an affiliate tracking cookie ensures our travel partner (e.g. Aviasales, Expedia, GetYourGuide) credits Getawayscout.com with the referral. This never increases the price you pay.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Anonymous analytical cookies that help us understand which destinations and travel articles are most popular so we can improve site speed and relevance.
  • Preference Cookies: Remembers your chosen search parameters (such as preferred departure airport or travel dates).
Affiliate Tracking Guarantee: Affiliate cookies placed during your search are used solely for referral verification by accredited travel networks. They do not store personal financial data, passwords, or credit card numbers.

3. Third-Party Partner Cookies

Some embedded search widgets and interactive widgets (such as Expedia search bars, KiwiTaxi booking frames, or Travelpayouts script widgets) may deploy their own third-party cookies governed by their respective privacy statements.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You have full control over your cookie settings. You can modify your browser preferences to accept, block, or delete cookies at any time:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and data stored

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect search widget responsiveness or prevent automated price comparisons from loading smoothly.

5. Policy Updates

We may update our Cookie Policy periodically to reflect technological changes or regulatory updates. Please review this page occasionally for the latest information.
