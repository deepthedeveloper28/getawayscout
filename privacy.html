Privacy Policy

At Getawayscout.com, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us while browsing our travel portal. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights under data protection laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

When you visit Getawayscout.com, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Voluntary Information: Email address provided when you subscribe to our newsletter or your name, email, and message details submitted through our contact form.
  • Automated Browsing Data: IP address, device type, browser characteristics, referring URLs, and interaction patterns gathered anonymously through cookies and analytics tags.
  • Travel Search Queries: Destination names, preferred flight dates, passenger counts, and hotel locations entered into comparison search widgets.
Payment Data Protection: Getawayscout.com does not request, process, or store credit card details or financial payment credentials. All payment processing occurs entirely on secure, encrypted third-party checkout gateways (e.g. Booking.com, Expedia, DiscoverCars, KiwiTaxi).

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Delivering search results and facilitating seamless redirects to official booking partners.
  • Sending curated travel deals, flash sales, and destination recommendations (only if you opted in to our newsletter).
  • Responding to customer feedback, support tickets, and inquiry forms.
  • Analyzing site performance, resolving technical errors, and improving overall user experience.

3. Third-Party Sharing & Affiliate Partners

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. When you interact with a search widget or affiliate link, basic query parameters (such as departure airport or travel dates) may be passed to partner networks (e.g. Travelpayouts, Expedia, GetYourGuide) to populate your booking request.

4. Data Retention & Security

We employ modern industry-standard security safeguards, including HTTPS SSL encryption and firewalls, to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure of your data. Newsletter subscriber data is securely retained until you choose to unsubscribe via the link in any email.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR / Data Protection Laws

Depending on your country of residence, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections or complete erasure of your data, or withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

6. Contact Our Data Privacy Officer

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data privacy rights, please contact us at support@getawayscout.com.
