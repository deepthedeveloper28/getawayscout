Under European Regulation (EC) No 261/2004, passengers are entitled to compensation ranging from €250 to €600 for flights delayed by 3+ hours, canceled within 14 days of departure, or in cases of denied boarding due to overbooking, provided the issue was within the airline's control.
In most jurisdictions, you can claim compensation for eligible disrupted flights that took place up to 3 to 6 years ago, depending on the country of departure or airline registry.
