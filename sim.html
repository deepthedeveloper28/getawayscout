How Travel eSIM Works

Connect to top local telecom carriers in 3 simple steps — no plastic SIM cards needed.

1

Choose Destination

Search for your destination country or region and choose the data package that fits your trip duration and data needs.

2

Install with QR Code

Receive your digital eSIM code via email instantly. Simply scan the QR code in your phone's Cellular/Mobile settings.

3

Arrive & Connect

When you land at your destination, turn on data roaming on your eSIM line. Enjoy seamless 4G/5G data straight away!
